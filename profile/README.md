We're a specialized consulting firm producing cutting-edge quantitative solutions to hard ocean science problems, based in Vancouver, Canada.

## What we do

- **MSE Software**: development of [openMSE](https://www.openMSE.com), an open-source framework that makes management strategy evaluation accessible for all fisheries
- **MSE Applications**: applying our framework to fisheries ranging from high-seas tuna stocks to nearshore invertebrates
- **Management Procedures**: design, development, and testing of custom harvest strategies
- **Stock Assessment**: development and application of assessment methods for data-limited and data-rich fisheries
- **Statistical Analysis**: environmental data analysis and bespoke population dynamics models
- **Interactive Apps**: fishery dashboards and web apps built with R Shiny
- **Training Courses**: customized courses in MSE, stock assessment, and advanced R programming
